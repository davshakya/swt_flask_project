Complete Wireless Equipment

One wireless setup for every building height.

Every SaleWell setup uses a wireless tank sensor node and a main controller near the pump. No long signal cable is needed between them.

Tank side Wireless Tank Sensor Node

A powered sensor node stays close to the tank and sends water-level readings directly to the main controller.

  • No long sensor signal cable
  • Accurate local level readings
  • No external Wi-Fi router required
Included hardware: tank sensor node • Compatible with every plan
Pump side Wireless Pump Controller

The main controller stays near the pump, receives tank readings wirelessly and manages automation and alerts.

  • Automatic and remote pump control
  • Fail-safe motor OFF on stale data
  • Relay/contactor safety integration
Included hardware: pump controller • Pump relay control is optional on home plans

Questions Before You Buy

Clear answers to common questions.

Project-specific commercial terms are confirmed in writing with the final quotation.

Which plan is right for me?

Use Find My Kit above. Choose Home Basic for hardware only, Home Control for local app access, and Home Cloud Pro for remote access and AI analytics.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. You can add phone access, pump control, access from anywhere, source-tank monitoring or extra tank sensors later.

Does it work without home Wi-Fi?

Yes. Home Basic and Home Control communicate directly and do not need a home Wi-Fi router or internet. Cloud and remote-access features do require internet.

Is installation included in the listed price?

Installation may be separate or bundled depending on the city and site. The final quotation clearly separates equipment, installation and optional work.

How long does installation take?

Timing depends on tank access, power readiness, pump-panel wiring and the number of devices. The expected installation window is confirmed after the site check.

What warranty do I receive?

Warranty coverage varies by kit and project scope. The applicable period, covered parts and service process are stated in the final quotation before purchase.

Is AMC available?

Maintenance and support packages can be quoted for managed and commercial sites. Coverage and pricing depend on device count and service scope.

Can I add more tanks later?

Yes. Extra wireless sensor nodes and source-tank monitoring are available, subject to the site and controller capacity.

Can apartments, schools and factories use it?

Yes. RWA Standard, Commercial AI Pro and Enterprise Modular are intended for shared tanks, multiple users, reports and larger deployments.

What power and sensors are required?

The tank sensor node and controller require suitable power. Sensor selection depends on tank type, mounting position and site conditions and is confirmed during the setup review.

What are delivery and return terms?

Dispatch timing, delivery scope, cancellation and return terms are provided with the final quotation because custom-configured and installed equipment may have different conditions.

When is support available?

Contact SaleWell at support@salewell.co.in or +91-8796452878. The support window and response commitment for managed projects are defined in the selected service scope.
