SaleWell Water Monitoring

Water tank monitoring for homes, apartments, hotels and commercial buildings.

SaleWell helps you monitor rooftop and underground tanks from your phone or browser. You can see current water level, check pump status, reduce overflow and respond faster when a site is running low on water.

  • View tank level and pump status without climbing to the roof.
  • Receive clearer warning signals before overflow, dry run or shortage conditions.
  • Simple login for customers, easy control for installers.
  • Prevent overflow, avoid dry runs, and save water and electricity..
