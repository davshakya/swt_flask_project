How It Works

From installation to daily use in 4 practical steps.

📦
Wireless sensor installed in the tank

A compact smart sensor node is placed at the overhead or main tank. It needs power, but no long signal wire is needed between the tank and main controller.

🔧
Tank readings travel wirelessly

Water-level readings go directly to the main controller without needing home Wi-Fi or an internet connection.

📱
Main controller handles pump safety

The main device stays near the pump/control area, receives tank readings wirelessly, and manages alerts, relay control and dashboard updates.

✔
Choose local or cloud app access

Home Control connects locally without an external router. Home Cloud Pro adds remote dashboards, history and AI through the internet.

Frequently Asked Questions
Does it work without Wi-Fi?

Local controller protections can continue according to the installed configuration, but remote dashboards, cloud history and mobile alerts require connectivity.

Can it work with an existing tank and pump?

Usually yes. The setup check confirms tank access, sensor position, pump controls, power and relay or contactor compatibility.

Is professional installation required?

Sensor placement and low-voltage setup are straightforward, while mains pump wiring must be completed or approved by a qualified electrician.

What happens during a power cut?

The controller restarts when power returns. Remote data is unavailable during the outage, and retained behavior depends on the selected hardware and configuration.

Does it support multiple tanks?

Yes. Multi-tank and source-tank designs are available for apartments, societies and managed properties after a site assessment.

Is there a warranty?

Warranty and installation terms vary by kit and project scope. The applicable coverage is shown with the final quotation before purchase.
