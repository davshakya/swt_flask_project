How It Works

From installation to daily use in 4 practical steps.

1
Wireless sensor installed in the tank

A compact smart sensor is placed at the overhead/main tank. It needs power, but no long signal wire is pulled from the tank to the main controller.

2
Tank data travels over Wi-Fi

Water level readings are sent wirelessly to the main pump/controller device through the site Wi-Fi network.

3
Main controller handles pump safety

The main device stays near the pump/control area, receives tank readings wirelessly, and manages alerts, relay control and dashboard updates.

4
Live dashboard on your phone

Open the app or web dashboard to check tank level, pump status, alerts and remote actions without climbing up to inspect the tank.
