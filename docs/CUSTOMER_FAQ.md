# SaleWell Customer FAQ

These answers are for the public website chatbot. Final price, compatibility,
installation scope, and availability must be confirmed by SaleWell.

## Company and product

### What is your company name?

Our company is SaleWell IoT Solutions. We provide smart water-tank monitoring,
pump-control, automation, mobile-app, and cloud solutions.

### What does SaleWell do?

SaleWell measures tank level and can add alerts, pump automation, local phone
access, remote monitoring, history, reports, and analytics by plan.

### How does the device work?

A sensor node measures tank level and sends it wirelessly to the main
controller near the pump. The controller displays status and applies configured
pump and safety rules.

### Does the system work without internet?

Local sensing and controller automation can work without cloud access. Remote
access, cloud history, reports, and internet commands require connectivity.

### Is home Wi-Fi required?

Home Basic and Home Control do not require home internet for their local
features. Cloud features require a suitable internet connection.

## Tank monitoring and pump operation

### How does overflow prevention work?

The sensor reports tank level to the controller. With approved automation, the
configured stop threshold requests pump shutdown before overflow.

### How does dry-run protection work?

It uses installed source-water sensing or configured availability logic to
prevent or stop pumping when the source is unavailable or safety data is invalid.

### Can I start and stop the pump from my phone?

Yes, when the selected plan and installed pump interface support it. Remote
control from outside the property additionally requires cloud connectivity.

### Will the pump remain safe if the server is offline?

Local controller safety and automation are designed to remain independent of
cloud availability. The cloud adds remote visibility and command routing.

### Why is a contactor required?

A high-current pump must not run directly through a small controller relay.
A correctly rated contactor safely switches the pump load.

### What happens when sensor data is missing?

The controller should use fail-safe behavior instead of assuming the pump is
safe to run. The sensor, connection, and calibration should be checked.

## Installation

### What information is required for installation?

Provide city, property type, tank count, tank height/capacity, pump rating and
location, panel details, power availability, and local or remote-access needs.

### Where is the tank sensor installed?

The sensor is mounted near the tank, facing the water surface and clear of
walls, inlet flow, ladders, float valves, and other obstructions.

### Who should install the pump wiring?

A qualified electrician should install mains-voltage wiring, protection,
contactor, manual controls, earthing, and enclosure components.

### Can SaleWell support underground and overhead tanks?

Supported configurations can monitor source/underground and destination/
overhead tanks. The final sensor and controller roles depend on the site survey.

### Can one system monitor multiple tanks?

Yes, supported commercial and modular configurations can use multiple sensor
and controller roles. The design depends on tank count and communication range.

## Troubleshooting

### Why is the tank level incorrect?

Check sensor angle, obstruction, condensation, tank-height calibration, inlet
flow, wireless freshness, and whether the sensor faces the water correctly.

### Why is the pump not starting?

Possible reasons include a full destination tank, unavailable source water,
missing sensor data, manual mode, safety lockout, or an electrical-panel issue.

### What should I do if the pump does not stop?

Use the physical OFF control or isolate power safely, then contact a qualified
electrician. Do not bypass protection to keep the pump running.

### Why is remote data stale?

Check controller power, site internet, cloud connectivity, and last-seen time.
Local control may continue even when remote updates are unavailable.

### Why can the app not connect locally?

Confirm controller power, intended phone connection, local permissions, and
the selected device. Local access and cloud login are different modes.
